# Core Premise

| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Genre          | Hard fantasy with light isekai elements                                                                                                                              |
| Core Concept   | A modern programmer/engineer is stranded in a fantasy world where magic is a deterministic natural phenomenon that has never undergone a true scientific revolution. |
| Central Hook   | Magic is treated like physics rather than mysticism. The protagonist introduces the scientific method, not modern technology itself.                                 |

## Arrival and Consequence Constraints

Sourceworld chronologies have no stable correspondence to time in the Expanse. This does not permit return, communication, or information to travel from the Expanse to a sourceworld: every observed Arrival is one-way from the perspective of its inhabitants. Whether an Arrival transfers a person or creates an otherwise inaccessible duplicate remains unknowable within the Expanse.

## Worldbuilding Principle

The setting aims for **realism in its consequences**. Given premises—especially deterministic magic, Arrivals, and the Expanse's unusual environment—their social, material, political, and historical effects should follow coherently from one another rather than accumulate as unrelated conventions.

Coherence is a constraint, not an instruction to exhaust every plausible implication. When several consequences are equally credible, prefer the one that is simpler to express, creates useful connections with established material, and is more interesting for the story.
