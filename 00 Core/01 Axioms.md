
# Setting Axioms

- Technology is not magic. Every important capability has operating conditions, material costs, failure modes, maintenance requirements, and limits that can be understood in principle.
- Faster-than-light travel exists, but is constrained enough that distance, travel time, navigation, access, and logistics still shape history and plot.
- No time travel, paradoxes, or causality-resetting solutions.
- The anomalous displacement is extraordinary but not a licence for arbitrary effects. Its observed consequences must be internally consistent and have discoverable limits.
- Intelligence, expertise, and goodwill are distributed unevenly. No people, species, or organisations are inherently pure evil, uniformly stupid, or perfectly rational.
- The future contains injustice, scarcity, grief, and institutional failure, but also real capacity for solidarity, reform, curiosity, and competent cooperation.
- Non-human people are people: biologically and culturally different, internally diverse, and entitled to moral consideration. Species is not a shortcut for a single culture or political viewpoint.
- The narrative may retain uncertainty, but authorial uncertainty must be marked as such rather than concealed as established fact.

## Intended Use

This file records the setting's durable non-negotiable constraints. Add an axiom only when changing it would substantially change many other documents; put more specific technical and social rules in their owning references.
