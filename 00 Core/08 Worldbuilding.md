# Recommended Build Order

0. Magic system ( basically done ) 
1. Environment & Physical Constraints
2. Resources & Economy
3. Institutions
4. Politics & Power Structures
5. Species
6. Cultures & Local Societies
7. States & Polities
8. Historical Development
9. Current Conflicts & Tensions

---

# 1. Environment & Physical Constraints

Define the world's recurring pressures before its civilisations.

Focus on:

- Geography
- Climate
- Resource distribution
- Mana distribution
- Natural hazards
- Ecology
- Travel & communication

> Civilisations emerge as solutions to environmental problems.

---

# 2. Resources & Economy

Determine what society depends upon.

Define:

- Valuable resources
- Production methods
- Transportation
- Trade
- Labour
- Infrastructure
- Scarcity
- Wealth generation

> Economy determines incentives.

---

# 3. Institutions

Design organisations before countries.

Examples of institution categories:

- Magi Colleges
- Trade Guilds
- Academies
- Document Archives
- Religious organisations
- Bureaucracies
- Military orders
- Financial institutions

For each institution define:

- Purpose
- Resources
- Authority
- Knowledge
- Dependencies
- Internal structure
- Long-term objectives

> Institutions shape civilization more than borders.

---

# 4. Politics & Power

Determine how authority is distributed.

Define:

- Sources of legitimacy
- Distribution of magical power
- Lawmaking
- Administration
- Succession
- Diplomacy
- Political factions

> Politics emerge from competing institutions.

---

# 5. Species

Design species around ecology rather than archetypes.

Define:

- Evolutionary niche
- Physiology
- Sensory differences
- Magical capabilities
- Limitations
- Demographics
- Social tendencies

Species should influence:

- Architecture
- Transportation
- Warfare
- Economy
- Daily life

> Species change civilization through practical consequences.

---

# 6. Cultures & Local Societies

Develop how people live.

Define:

- Values
- Customs
- Education
- Family structures
- Languages
- Religion
- Traditions
- Professions

> Culture emerges from environment, institutions and history.

---

# 7. States & Polities

Build political entities from existing institutions.

Define:

- Territory
- Administrative divisions
- Dominant institutions
- Alliances
- Rivalries
- External relations

> Countries are coalitions of competing interests.

---

# 8. Historical Development

Construct history as accumulated change.

Focus on:

- Technological shifts
- Magical discoveries
- Major migrations
- Arrivals
- Wars
- Schisms
- Institutional evolution

> History explains the present rather than existing for its own sake.

---

# 9. Conflicts & Tensions

Every major element should generate conflict.

For each organization, society or polity define:

- Goals
- Resources
- Dependencies
- Rivals
- Internal disagreements
- External pressures

> Stable worlds are built from unstable relationships.

---

# General Principles

- Build causes before outcomes.
- Let civilizations emerge from constraints.
- Let institutions shape politics.
- Let species solve ecological niches.
- Let economics drive incentives.
- Let history accumulate naturally.
- Prefer ideological conflicts over purely moral ones.
- Design interactions before isolated factions.
- Every major system should influence several others.
- Every worldbuilding decision should create downstream consequences.
