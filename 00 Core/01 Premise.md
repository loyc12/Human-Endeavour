**Type: Core reference**

**Status: Current foundation**

## Established Constraints

- The [[03 Ship and Crew/00 Ship and Mission|pilot project vessel]] is a human-led coalition project with significant non-human participation, mixed civilian and enlisted personnel, and provision for family and clan ties. Its integrated habitation programme is earnest, controversial, and imperfect.
- The vessel was not sent to Andromeda intentionally.
- Most known sapient species share an entangled history with human expansion or uplift. One [[02 World/01 Milky Way/05 Independent Near-Peer Civilisation|independently spacefaring civilisation]] is the only fully contacted near-peer of human space; its diaspora and continuing polities are part of the mission context.
- The displacement severely damages the ship, causes heavy loss of life, and removes the original captain from effective command. The captain's ultimate fate remains open.
- The ship passes through a spatial tear in which violent motion and debris cause much of the damage. The tear's mechanism, debris source, and connection to the destination wreckage field remain open.
- The anomaly is a physical phenomenon whose effects must obey the setting axioms, even if the crew cannot initially explain or reproduce it.
- The crew cannot rely on timely help, resupply, or ordinary communication from home. The precise communications and causality boundary is still to be defined.
- The vessel's advanced systems confer options, not immunity from fuel, damage, maintenance, information gaps, social strain, or ethical responsibility.
- Andromeda contains civilisations with more disparate origins and less shared history than the Milky Way. Its societies are not presumed to be technologically, biologically, morally, or politically uniform.
- A direct return through ordinary FTL would be a multigenerational, multi-millennia undertaking. Finding an alternative is therefore preferable, but must not be easy or consequence-free.

## Worldbuilding Principle

The setting aims for **realism in its consequences**. Given premises ( especially the constrained FTL, the ship's finite capabilities, the anomalous displacement, and independent civilisations ) their social, material, political, and historical effects should flow coherently from one another rather than accumulate as unrelated conventions.

Coherence is a constraint rather than an instruction to exhaust every plausible implication. When several consequences are equally credible, prefer the one that is simpler to express, creates useful connections with established material, and is more interesting for the story.

## Open Foundations

The active order of work and explicit deferrals are in [[99 Workshop/00 Revision Priorities|Revision Priorities]].

The following questions are intentionally unresolved and should be answered before detailed technical, political, or historical canon depends on them:

- What is the coalition called, what authority does it have, and what legal mandate and safeguards govern the pilot programme?
- What was the programme's actual duration and risk profile, and how do civilian, enlisted, family, and clan relationships work aboard it?
- What did the anomaly do besides displacement, and why does its exit contain a wreckage field?
- How do FTL travel, interstellar communication, navigation, and energy supply work, and which limitations make the return journey difficult rather than impossible?
- Which Andromedan civilisations or historical paths make the first region compelling and distinct from the Milky Way?
- What does a credible route home require, and what moral or strategic costs could pursuing it impose?
