# Arrival Region

**Type: Setting reference**

**Status: Provisional**

**Development: Explicitly deferred until resumed by the author**

## Endpoint Proposal

The [[03 Ship and Crew/00 Ship and Mission|ship]] may emerge in a region that functions as a frequent endpoint for spatial anomalies. It is primarily a dense, unusual field of naturally accumulated matter, but contains a noticeable minority of artificial structures and wreckage captured from elsewhere.

The region could be valuable to scavengers because the anomalies concentrate accessible material from otherwise distant locations. Competition for that material may have produced some of the artificial debris and evidence of conflict, but should not make the field chiefly a battlefield or a convenient storehouse of everything the ship needs.

Its relation to a small star, rogue planetoid, or ordinary interstellar space; its age; and the mechanism that localises anomalies there are unresolved. The poor prior documentation of the anomalies must eventually have a credible explanation.

## Deferred Questions

When this topic resumes, first determine whether the field is gravitationally bound, transient, or engineered; then establish exit velocity, debris age and provenance, discovery history, and the economic reasons for scavenging. The endpoint mechanism and scavenger economy must not be used to solve the ship's logistics while they remain deferred.

The [[00 Core/01 Premise|premise]] owns the established displacement constraints. [[99 Workshop/00 Revision Priorities|Revision Priorities]] records the deferral; it is not an instruction to develop the region now.
