# Milky Way Overview

**Type: Setting reference**

**Status: Current foundation**

This page introduces the civilisation the crew comes from. It owns only the broad social expectations below; the linked references own historical, geographical, and contact detail.

## What the Crew Treats as Normal

The [[00 Core/01 Premise|Milky Way mission context]] is not socially uniform, but ordinary interstellar travel is sufficiently mature and low-conflict that many long missions are planned as sustained communities rather than brief military deployments. Civilian work, family or clan ties, recreation, and routine maintenance are normal parts of life aboard a major vessel.

Large projects are ordinarily expected to conclude within participants' lifetimes. A multi-generational return voyage is therefore not merely long; it is a profound departure from the social expectations under which the crew enlisted or volunteered.

The political environment, economic distribution, and degree of material abundance remain open. Do not infer a universal post-scarcity society from the relative routine of space travel.
