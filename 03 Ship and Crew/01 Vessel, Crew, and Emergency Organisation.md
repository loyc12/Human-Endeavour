# Vessel, Crew, and Emergency Organisation

**Type: Setting reference**

**Status: Exploring**

**Approval: Required before canonisation**

This working reference combines the `DR Ship - Info.tsv` snapshot with ship alternatives from older HSC notes. It preserves numerical, organisational, and narrative material without resolving draft conflicts. The established programme is in [[03 Ship and Crew/00 Ship and Mission|Ship and Mission]].

## Vessel Design Alternatives

- A large cylindrical vessel may separate habitation, cargo/reserves, and propulsion into broad longitudinal zones.
- Older notes give a kilometre-scale vessel; *Drifting Refuge* gives a 415-metre ship. The size must be selected alongside crew complement, cargo needs, damage scale, and narrative atmosphere.
- The project may have first used localised gravimetric fields in much of its work environment, while retaining rotating sections where appropriate.

The intended distinction is that a quarter can remain structurally functional while being inaccessible, insufficiently supplied, unheated, contaminated, under repair, or otherwise not active for habitation.

## Command Situation

- The original captain is critically injured in the anomaly. Current narrative preference is that they die after an interval.
- Tomas, the wounded human first officer, becomes acting captain and is the intended green captain viewpoint.
- The original second officer is killed.
- Ava'an, an Évbé'iL third officer, is a candidate for promotion to executive officer and the intended still-greener XO viewpoint.
- The primary bridge crew is largely or wholly lost, forcing command to operate from an emergency or secondary command space.

## Institutional Survivors

The draft includes a civilian overseer elected by civilians, legal counsel, moral counsellor, diplomatic counsel, medical and engineering leadership, scientific leadership, security, logistics, and tactical staff. Several departmental heads are killed or injured.

This supports a ship that remains a mixed civic institution during crisis rather than becoming a simple military hierarchy. The exact authority of each office, emergency succession law, and relationship between civilian representation and command remain unresolved.

## Departmental Effects

- Medical, engineering, logistics, tactical, scientific, and command teams all survive in reduced form.
- The death or injury of senior specialists creates bottlenecks in care, repair, governance, and knowledge transfer—not merely a lower labour count.
- The lower-deck ensemble can encounter command decisions through crowded quarters, repair work, medical triage, rationing, informal support, and changing work assignments.

## Approval Questions

- What vessel length, broad layout, and gravity arrangement suit the complement, reserves, damage scale, and story?
- Is 146 the final death count, or does the prose figure of 147 better serve the story?
- What is the exact interval and circumstance of the former captain's death?
- Does Ava'an become XO, or does another survivor fill that role?
- Which civilian offices have real emergency authority, and which are advisory only?
- Which roles belong to the future lower-deck viewpoint group?

## Operational State to Define

Record which gravity zones, radiator capacity, power systems, manufacturing capability, sensors, propulsion, and stores survive the displacement. The individual references own the technology; this vessel reference owns what is installed, available, damaged, or inaccessible aboard this ship.

[[01 Technologies/01 Key Concepts/02 Metric-Gradient Gravity Plating|Gravity plating]], [[01 Technologies/02 Devices and Applications/02 Thermal Battery Banks|thermal storage]], and [[01 Technologies/02 Devices and Applications/03 Emergency Vacuum Protection and Repair|vacuum protection and repair]] constrain these decisions. The opening must not assume a survival capability simply because it exists elsewhere in the setting.

The emergency-authority model must distinguish command, civilian representation, legal review, medical authority, advisory offices, appeal, and the resumption of civilian governance. Precise powers remain open.
