### Spacetime Engineering and Survival

- **Approve the technology family boundary.** Decide the minimum common physics behind jump travel, gravity plating, inertial management, and any FTL communications; retire entanglement terminology from active use.
  Source: [[99 Workshop/02 Spacetime Engineering|Spacetime Engineering]].
- **Selected travel-scale baseline.** The balanced model fixes an eight-thousand-year history, FTL discovery at 260 BCD, a 0.05c pre-FTL diaspora, a delayed rise to 150c mature corridors and 500c exceptional service, and distinct route, settlement, and exploration extents. Do not reopen these figures unless a selected FTL mechanism or direct narrative requirement contradicts them.
  Source: [[99 Workshop/06 Space Travel Scaling Model|Space Travel Scaling Model]].
- **Develop the beam-array corridor layer.** System-scale beam arrays are an active extrapolative-engineering direction: a potential explanation for rare early `0.1c` foundation voyages, an institutional precursor to high-value corridors, and an enduring parallel network for intra-system logistics and slow uncrewed interstellar resource packets without onboard FTL equipment. Decide their scale, access, range, braking arrangement, and historical role without inferring an FTL mechanism.
  Source: [[01 Technologies/01 Key Concepts/01 System-Scale Beam Arrays|System-Scale Beam Arrays]].
- **Set the immediate operational limits.** Decide which gravity-plating zones, thermal-radiator capacity, power systems, manufacturing capacity, sensors, and propulsion elements survive the displacement. This determines what the opening crisis can honestly demand.
  Sources: [[01 Technologies/02 Devices Descriptions/02 Thermal Battery Banks|Thermal Battery Banks]], [[01 Technologies/02 Devices Descriptions/03 Emergency Vacuum Protection and Repair|Emergency Vacuum Protection and Repair]].
- **Resolve home-contact logic.** If FTL communications exist, define why the ship has no usable path home; if they do not, remove them from future technology options.
  Sources: [[00 Core/03 Premise|Premise]], [[99 Workshop/02 Spacetime Engineering|Spacetime Engineering]].

## Priority 3 — Foundation for Later Arcs

### Milky Way Context

- **Selected baseline — broad historical spine, pre-FTL hierarchy, topology, and spatial scale.** The setting is about eight thousand years old; its clustered 0.05c sublight diaspora reaches a characteristic 390 ly, with rare 0.1c first-order foundations, four to seven legacy civilisation-complexes, smaller later clusters, and a mixed outer fringe. Human-touched space forms a core-weighted web of dense clusters, high-value corridors, midway settlements, local meshes, sparse branches, and isolated nodes; mapped, reachable, settled, and integrated space are distinct categories. FTL begins at 260 BCD; routine short travel emerges around 100 BCD; and the true core, old diaspora, reconnection settlement belt, sparse frontier, and exploration corridors have selected working scales. Do not re-litigate these figures unless a selected FTL mechanism or direct narrative requirement contradicts them.
  Sources: [[02 World/01 History/00 Broad Historical Timeline|Broad Historical Timeline]], [[02 World/01 History/04 Pre-FTL Diaspora Geography|Pre-FTL Diaspora Geography]], [[02 World/01 History/05 Settlement Topology and Connectivity|Settlement Topology and Connectivity]].
- **Selected baseline — uplift and contact horizon.** Multiple uneven uplift histories exist. One advanced independently spacefaring near-peer was contacted about 190 BCD, has undergone post-contact technological acceleration and integration, and has a small but geographically dispersed diaspora beyond its own polities. Rare non-spacefaring independent societies occur more often in the wider frontier; any further technological detections remain remote and recent. Preserve agency while later defining particular demographies, polities, and contact practice.
  Sources: [[02 World/01 History/01 Uplift, Adaptation, and Sovereignty|Uplift, Adaptation, and Sovereignty]], [[02 World/01 History/02 Independent Technological Civilisations|Independent Technological Civilisations]], [[02 World/01 History/03 Technological Horizons and Contact History|Technological Horizons and Contact History]].
- **Retain only the timeline's genuinely open dependencies.** The origin and early control of FTL, exact settlement and population counts, the detailed operation of communications and high-speed routes, and the coalition's constitutional structure remain open; resolve them only when the FTL, demographic, and political questions that constrain them are reached.
  Sources: [[99 Workshop/02 Spacetime Engineering|Spacetime Engineering]], [[99 Workshop/05 Milky Way Astropolitical Foundations|Astropolitical Foundations]].
- **Selected baseline — spatial continuity of settlement.** Human-touched space is a nested archipelago of dense regional clusters and self-reinforcing corridors, with midway settlements, local route meshes, thin branches, and genuine gaps shaped by settlement interest and astrography. Do not ascribe those gaps to a particular FTL constraint before the FTL model is selected.
  Source: [[02 World/01 History/05 Settlement Topology and Connectivity|Settlement Topology and Connectivity]].
- Develop the remaining first-order civilisational model before detailed states or institutions through the consolidated decision packages: FTL-enabled reconnection; material and technological capacity; automation and artificial persons; bodies and habitation; FTL-era spatial development; population geography; convergence; ordinary life and resilience; governance and power; economic integration; and present structural tensions. Keep results conditional on FTL, navigation, communications, and energy constraints.
  Source: [[99 Workshop/05 Milky Way Astropolitical Foundations|Astropolitical Foundations]].
