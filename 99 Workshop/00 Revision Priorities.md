## Priority 1 — Needed Before Detailed Opening Drafts

### Command and Ship Society

- **Set the succession chain.** Confirm the former captain's delayed death, Tomas as green captain, and whether Ava'an becomes XO. Resolve the second officer's death and the formal authority of acting ranks.
  Sources: [[99 Workshop/01 Drifting Refuge Ship and Crew Snapshot|Ship and Crew Snapshot]], [[04 Narrative/01 Narrative Foundation|Narrative Foundation]].
- **Define emergency legitimacy.** State which powers the captain, civilian overseer, legal counsel, medical leadership, and other offices hold during catastrophe; include review, appeal, and resumption of civilian governance.
  Source: [[99 Workshop/01 Drifting Refuge Ship and Crew Snapshot|Ship and Crew Snapshot]].
- **Choose a practical ship baseline.** Set final vessel length, broad layout, initial complement, death total, active quarters, and the meaning of functional versus active spaces. Present working figures are 415 metres versus one kilometre, and 146 versus 147 deaths.
  Sources: [[99 Workshop/01 Drifting Refuge Ship and Crew Snapshot|Ship and Crew Snapshot]], [[99 Workshop/03 Historical and Character Seeds|Historical and Character Seeds]].

### Spacetime Engineering and Survival

- **Approve the technology family boundary.** Decide the minimum common physics behind jump travel, gravity plating, inertial management, and any FTL communications; retire entanglement terminology from active use.
  Source: [[99 Workshop/02 Spacetime Engineering|Spacetime Engineering]].
- **Selected travel-scale baseline.** The balanced model fixes an eight-thousand-year history, FTL discovery at 260 BCD, a 0.05c pre-FTL diaspora, a delayed rise to 150c mature corridors and 500c exceptional service, and distinct route, settlement, and exploration extents. Do not reopen these figures unless a selected FTL mechanism or direct narrative requirement contradicts them.
  Source: [[99 Workshop/06 Space Travel Scaling Model|Space Travel Scaling Model]].
- **Set the immediate operational limits.** Decide which gravity-plating zones, thermal-radiator capacity, power systems, manufacturing capacity, sensors, and propulsion elements survive the displacement. This determines what the opening crisis can honestly demand.
  Sources: [[01 Technologies/02 Devices Descriptions/02 Thermal Battery Banks|Thermal Battery Banks]], [[01 Technologies/02 Devices Descriptions/03 Emergency Vacuum Protection and Repair|Emergency Vacuum Protection and Repair]].
- **Resolve home-contact logic.** If FTL communications exist, define why the ship has no usable path home; if they do not, remove them from future technology options.
  Sources: [[00 Core/03 Premise|Premise]], [[99 Workshop/02 Spacetime Engineering|Spacetime Engineering]].

## Priority 3 — Foundation for Later Arcs

### Milky Way Context

- **Selected baseline — broad historical spine and spatial scale.** The setting is about eight thousand years old; its 0.05c sublight diaspora reaches about 390 ly; FTL begins at 260 BCD; routine short travel emerges around 100 BCD; and the true core, old diaspora, reconnection settlement belt, sparse frontier, and exploration corridors have selected working scales. Do not re-litigate these figures unless a selected FTL mechanism or direct narrative requirement contradicts them.
  Source: [[02 World/01 History/00 Broad Historical Timeline|Broad Historical Timeline]].
- **Selected baseline — uplift and independent contact.** Multiple uneven uplift histories exist; exactly one independently technological alien civilisation was contacted about 150 BCD, has undergone negotiated post-contact technological and civilisational uplift, and has a small but geographically dispersed diaspora beyond its own polities. Preserve independent origin and agency while later defining detailed demographic distribution and political institutions.
  Sources: [[02 World/01 History/01 Uplift, Adaptation, and Sovereignty|Uplift, Adaptation, and Sovereignty]], [[02 World/01 History/02 Independent Technological Civilisations|Independent Technological Civilisations]].
- **Retain only the timeline's genuinely open dependencies.** The origin and early control of FTL, the scale of settled space, and the coalition's constitutional structure remain open; resolve them only when the FTL, spatial, and political questions that constrain them are reached.
  Sources: [[99 Workshop/02 Spacetime Engineering|Spacetime Engineering]], [[99 Workshop/05 Milky Way Astropolitical Foundations|Astropolitical Foundations]].
- Develop the first-order civilisational model before detailed states or institutions: reconnection, technological and industrial capacity, mundane life, settlement and logistics patterns, spatial structure, population and lineage composition, convergence, power balance, economic integration, Core--Rim distribution, and present structural tensions. Keep results conditional on FTL, navigation, communications, and energy constraints.
  Source: [[99 Workshop/05 Milky Way Astropolitical Foundations|Astropolitical Foundations]].

### Andromeda and First Contact

- Build only the first reachable region and its first society or societies, after the ship's operational limits are known.
- Specify biology, communication, institutions, material conditions, internal disagreement, and the asymmetric risks of contact before creating a galaxy-wide political map.
  Source: [[00 Core/06 Worldbuilding|Worldbuilding Order]].
