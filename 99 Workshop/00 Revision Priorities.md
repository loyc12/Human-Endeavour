## Priority 1 — Needed Before Detailed Opening Drafts

### Command and Ship Society

- **Set the succession chain.** Confirm the former captain's delayed death, Tomas as green captain, and whether Ava'an becomes XO. Resolve the second officer's death and the formal authority of acting ranks.  
  Sources: [[99 Workshop/01 Drifting Refuge Ship and Crew Snapshot|Ship and Crew Snapshot]], [[04 Narrative/01 Narrative Foundation|Narrative Foundation]].
- **Define emergency legitimacy.** State which powers the captain, civilian overseer, legal counsel, medical leadership, and other offices hold during catastrophe; include review, appeal, and resumption of civilian governance.  
  Source: [[99 Workshop/01 Drifting Refuge Ship and Crew Snapshot|Ship and Crew Snapshot]].
- **Choose a practical ship baseline.** Set final vessel length, broad layout, initial complement, death total, active quarters, and the meaning of functional versus active spaces. Present working figures are 415 metres versus one kilometre, and 146 versus 147 deaths.  
  Sources: [[99 Workshop/01 Drifting Refuge Ship and Crew Snapshot|Ship and Crew Snapshot]], [[99 Workshop/03 Historical and Character Seeds|Historical and Character Seeds]].

### Spacetime Engineering and Survival

- **Approve the technology family boundary.** Decide the minimum common physics behind jump travel, gravity plating, inertial management, and any FTL communications; retire entanglement terminology from active use.  
  Source: [[99 Workshop/02 Spacetime Engineering|Spacetime Engineering]].
- **Set the immediate operational limits.** Decide which gravity-plating zones, thermal-radiator capacity, power systems, manufacturing capacity, sensors, and propulsion elements survive the displacement. This determines what the opening crisis can honestly demand.  
  Sources: [[01 Technologies/02 Devices Descriptions/02 Thermal Battery Banks|Thermal Battery Banks]], [[01 Technologies/02 Devices Descriptions/03 Emergency Vacuum Protection and Repair|Emergency Vacuum Protection and Repair]].
- **Resolve home-contact logic.** If FTL communications exist, define why the ship has no usable path home; if they do not, remove them from future technology options.  
  Sources: [[00 Core/03 Premise|Premise]], [[99 Workshop/02 Spacetime Engineering|Spacetime Engineering]].

## Priority 2 — Needed for the First Recovery Arc

### Narrative and Characters

- **Select the primary lower-deck ensemble.** Choose their jobs, relationships, species/access needs, personal pressures, and direct stakes in the emergency polity. Preserve existing character seeds only after resolving name, profession, and identity overlaps.  
  Sources: [[04 Narrative/01 Narrative Foundation|Narrative Foundation]], [[99 Workshop/03 Historical and Character Seeds|Historical and Character Seeds]].
- **Choose the report framing.** Decide who authors the interrupted evaluation report, its institutional audience, and whether it later has political consequences.  
  Source: [[99 Workshop/04 Opening and Early Arc Seeds|Opening and Early Arc Seeds]].
- **Plan the recovery sequence.** Turn the existing five-step early arc into beats with concrete choices, costs, reversals, and lower-deck consequences.  
  Source: [[99 Workshop/04 Opening and Early Arc Seeds|Opening and Early Arc Seeds]].

### Arrival Region — Explicitly Deferred

- **Do not develop the anomaly endpoint mechanism or scavenger economy yet.** The current endpoint-region model remains provisional and should not be used to solve logistics until the user resumes this topic.  
  Source: [[02 World/General Ideas|World General Ideas]].
- When resumed, first decide whether the field is gravitationally bound, transient, or engineered; then determine exit velocity, debris age and provenance, discovery history, and the economic reasons for scavenging.

## Priority 3 — Foundation for Later Arcs

### Milky Way Context

- Select the historical spine: approximate date, duration of sublight diaspora, age and origin of fast FTL, scale of settled space, and the coalition's constitutional structure.  
  Sources: [[03 Society/General Ideas|Society General Ideas]], [[99 Workshop/03 Historical and Character Seeds|Historical and Character Seeds]].
- Define the uplift history with attention to consent, sovereignty, dependency, and the independent technological species' continuing agency.  
  Sources: [[00 Core/07 Terminology|Terminology]], [[99 Workshop/03 Historical and Character Seeds|Historical and Character Seeds]].

### Andromeda and First Contact

- Build only the first reachable region and its first society or societies, after the ship's operational limits are known.
- Specify biology, communication, institutions, material conditions, internal disagreement, and the asymmetric risks of contact before creating a galaxy-wide political map.  
  Source: [[00 Core/06 Worldbuilding|Worldbuilding Order]].
