# Opening and Early Arc Seeds

**Status: Exploring**  
**Approval: Required before canonisation**

This page extracts potentially compatible material from `FAR.md`, `Far Storyboard.md`, `Datamai's Journey.md`, and a later opening-fragment draft. It does not adopt their older political history, character names, ship scale, or technical explanations as canon.

## Framing Device: The Interrupted Evaluation Report

Before the anomaly, an officer prepares an internal or governmental report evaluating the pilot vessel's ordinary operation, crew morale and social integration, and the effectiveness of its multispecies accommodations. The report establishes that the project is an earnest, imperfect experiment rather than a purely symbolic gesture.

The anomaly interrupts this mundane work. The people who were being assessed must now demonstrate whether their shared systems, training, and relationships can survive catastrophe.

This can introduce the vessel's purpose and human-default compromises through a character's concrete work, rather than through encyclopaedic exposition.

### Questions

- Who writes the report: a junior command officer, a civilian representative, a sociologist, an external evaluator, or several authors?
- Was the report expected to decide future funding, replication of the ship model, or only continuing certification?
- Does its unfinished or recovered text later become evidence in political conflict, grief, or reform?

## Command, Mentorship, and Succession

Older drafts pair a capable senior captain with a conscientious but inexperienced junior officer. The senior's mentorship can make their later injury and death emotionally consequential while leaving the green first officer as the credible successor.

The current ship snapshot instead identifies Tomas as first officer, the second officer as dead, and Ava'an as a candidate XO. These concepts can coexist if the former captain had been actively mentoring Tomas before the anomaly and Ava'an inherits an unexpectedly senior role.

## Early Arc: Restore a Community

1. The spatial disturbance overwhelms navigation, damage control, and ordinary command routines.
2. The surviving command team escapes, hides, triages casualties, limits heat production, and preserves a viable structural core.
3. Senior staff establish emergency authority, publish a truthful but stabilising statement, and reorganise shifts, accommodation, training, and care.
4. The ship becomes physically safer but remains socially exhausted: shortages, bereavement, lost expertise, overcrowding, and doubts about interim leadership persist.
5. The crew must choose whether to risk controlled scavenging, reconnaissance, or contact while still vulnerable.

The point of the early arc is not simply that repairs advance. The intended multispecies community must become a functioning emergency polity under conditions its designers did not anticipate.

## Useful Scene-Level Pressures

- Loss of gravity plating or inertial support can turn a familiar corridor into an immediate hazard without requiring the entire ship to lose gravity permanently.
- Heat-rejection failure forces a choice among power reduction, compartment sacrifice, use of temporary heat sinks, repair, concealment, and jettisoning equipment.
- An energy-bank detonation can serve as a decoy or distraction, but should be a conspicuous thermal and electromagnetic event, not invisible "thermal cover."
- A primary bridge exposed to vacuum can leave a captain critically injured, senior staff missing, and an emergency command space suddenly central.
- Spatial geometry that makes ordinary distance, velocity, and direction unreliable is a promising anomaly effect, but its formal mechanism remains postponed.

## Relationship and Care Boundary

Older storyboard material suggests an intimate reliance between a new captain and a newly promoted subordinate who shares disrupted accommodation after the disaster. The emotional material is useful: grief, fatigue, trust, difference in customs, loneliness, and professional support.

Their direct command relationship, emergency dependence, and housing scarcity create a serious power imbalance. If a romantic or sexual direction is retained, it should not be treated as casual shipboard inevitability. Establish meaningful consent, independent support, non-retaliatory options, and a clear handling of the chain of command; alternatively, keep the relationship as intimate friendship and mutual care.

## Historical Material Not Yet Compatible

`FAR.md`'s liberation from an alien empire, universal species census, named member-state counts, and Senate/UCAF structure belong to an older setting model. They should remain alternative material unless the current human-diaspora and uplift history is deliberately replaced.

`Datamai's Journey.md`'s serf-escape story is a separate premise. Its relevant transferable question is how ostensibly advanced societies can create material dependency and political domination without reducing every institution or character to a simple villain.

## Intended Use

Use this document when planning the opening and first recovery arc. Promote a choice only after it is reconciled with the crew snapshot, narrative foundation, and postponed anomaly decisions.
