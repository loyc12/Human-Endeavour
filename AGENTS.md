# Human Endeavour Project

This is an Obsidian-based hard-science-fiction worldbuilding and writing project.

- Follow the user's authorized scope. Help develop, organize, and audit author-facing documentation without silently changing canon. Preserve approval boundaries, uncertainty, provenance, and explicit deferrals.
- Validate changed material and affected dependencies only by default. Do not run full-project checks or repeat passing checks without relevant new edits, unresolved failures, new evidence, or an explicit audit request. Batch related edits and reuse checks across workflow steps.
- Agents and LLMs must not directly write, rewrite, or otherwise edit corpus prose, including drafts and equivalent creative text stored elsewhere, as those are meant to be fully user generated and maintained. However, they may read and discuss that material only when requested, providing critique or suggestions without modifying the source.
- Do not alter or mention the `.obsidian` workspace state folder. Ignore it and its content.
- Treat Legacy folders as obsolete. Consult them only when explicitly needed for the task.
- Start with [Start Here](Start%20Here.md) to locate a subject and its current owner.
- Before editing or auditing documentation, read the relevant sections of [Documentation Conventions](Documentation%20Conventions.md) and [Writing Style](Writing%20Style.md). Use Canadian English and the local reference voice.
- For development or work priority, consult [Development Method and Order](99%20Workshop/01%20Development%20Method%20and%20Order.md) and [Revision Priorities](99%20Workshop/00%20Revision%20Priorities.md) as needed.
