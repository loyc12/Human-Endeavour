# Narrative Foundation

**Status: Current narrative direction.** This document records present story preferences that may be revised without changing the setting's broader canon.

## Primary Viewpoints

The story follows both the [[00 Core/03 Premise|ship's]] new command team and a small, close-knit lower-deck crew. This permits the catastrophe and its consequences to be experienced at different scales: command decisions, their implementation, ordinary survival, friendship, resentment, and the culture that forms between them.

### Command Team

- The incoming captain is green and must grow into command under extraordinary conditions.
- Their executive officer is even less experienced, creating both a close working relationship and an asymmetry of responsibility.
- Current preferred direction: the former captain is fatally injured in the anomaly and dies after an interval rather than immediately. This gives their death visible emotional and institutional consequences while clearing the chain of command.

### Lower-Deck Ensemble

- A small recurring group provides a grounded view of repairs, accommodation, labour, relationships, and the effects of command choices.
- Other recurring characters may include a bartender whose low mood, social role, and access to informal information make them a meaningful part of shipboard life rather than only comic colour.

## Intended Use

This is a narrative-planning foundation, not a character sheet. Add specific protagonists, arcs, and episode functions after their roles within the ship's emerging society are decided.
