# Core Thesis
Civilisations advance not merely through intelligence or powerful discoveries, but through the patient construction of reliable knowledge, open collaboration, and relationships built on mutual understanding.

# Secondary Ideas
## Magic is physics
Magic is empirically knowable and materially manipulable, making it a powerful tool when properly researched and understood

## People are not stupid
Characters and organisations have discoverable goals, preferences, and incentives, and act towards fulfilling them to the best of their abilities and knowledge

## Organisations are not monoliths
Organisations and polities are made of people and their relations, and thus host internal tensions and divergent incentives, which inform their observable behaviour

## Ideas are born out of conditions
Cultures and ideologies are shaped by material conditions, generating selection pressure based on their suitability to the societies that contains them
